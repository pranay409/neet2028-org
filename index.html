3-Year Foundation Plan

What to Do in Each Phase of Your Preparation

The students who score above 650 in NEET are usually the ones who started early and stayed consistent. Here is how to structure those years.

Class 10 / Pre-Class 11 Phase

This is not the time to start NEET-specific content. It is the time to ensure your Class 10 Science foundation is genuinely solid. Mathematics, basic Chemistry bonding, and human biology at Class 10 level are the building blocks. Rushing into NEET material before this foundation is ready is a common mistake.

Class 11 Phase

Class 11 is where NEET prep properly begins. Chapters from Class 11 account for roughly 45 percent of the paper. Physics Mechanics, Cell Biology, Genetics, Organic Chemistry basics, and Thermodynamics need to be covered properly, not raced through. Enrol in a coaching that gives you personal guidance at this stage.

Class 12 and Final Phase

Class 12 chapters and revision of Class 11 together form the final lap. By January of your exam year, you should be in pure revision and mock test mode. Three to four full-length mocks per week, detailed error analysis, and weekly mentor sessions to course-correct your strategy are the tools for this phase.
